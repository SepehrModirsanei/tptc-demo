Kids Summer Camp 2026: registration opens February 16 with a super early bird special

Summer tennis camp registration (ages 4-17) opens February 16, with a super early bird special from February 16 at 10am to February 23 at 5pm, and prizes every week.

Posted
Jan 13, 2026
Updated
Feb 22, 2026
Written by
Thornhill Park Tennis Club

Registration closed It closed on Sep 4, 2026.

See Summer Camps

Exciting news! Summer tennis camp (ages 4-17) registration opens on February 16!

We are offering a super early bird special from February 16 at 10am until February 23 at 5pm! Go to our Camps page for more information on our various camp levels and programs.

Prizes every week

We will be offering amazing prizes, including Nike products and weekly racquet giveaways at the end of every week, and one lucky camper will win National Bank Open tennis tickets to see the pros play!

Three locations

Camp locations are listed in this order: (1) Yonge and Centre, (2) North Thornhill and (3) Garnet Williams.

How to register

  1. Go to Camps, then Summer Camps 2026, and choose the Summer camp online registration button.
  2. New to our camps? Choose First time camp registration: it creates your account in JBI, our online booking system. Taken camps with us before? Log in to your JBI account.
  3. In JBI, choose Program registration, then Camps in the left-hand menu, and tick Select beside each camp you want.
  4. Agree to the terms and conditions, choose Enroll and pay. You will receive an email confirmation with a receipt shortly after registration.
