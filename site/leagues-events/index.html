Early rounds
An 8-game superset, a 7-point tiebreak at 7-7. A Doubles and A Mixed play best of 3 sets.
Semi-finals and finals
Best of 3 sets, the third a 10-point tiebreak.
Warm-up
At most 7 minutes before a superset, 10 before a best of 3.
Late, early rounds
10 minutes: no warm-up. 15: lose a game and you serve. 20: lose 3 games and you serve. 30: lose the match.
Late, best of 3
10 minutes: no warm-up. 25: lose the first set. 40: lose the match.
Rescheduling
Agree a new time with your opponent and book the court yourself, before the next round. If you cannot, you are defaulted.
Rain
Resume from the score where play stopped, before the next round. If players cannot agree a time, both are disqualified.
The final
Played before the Dome goes up, and may be scheduled for AGM Sunday.

Before you join

Two questions players ask.

The leagues are set by playing level, from 3.0 to 5.5; a higher number is a stronger player. Team letters work the same way: Majors is the toughest intercounty division, then A, B and C.

Club to confirmThe club’s own wording for its level scaleThe club uses the numbers and letters without explaining them. We will publish the club’s description, and how a new player is placed.

The club states it for some: the summer house leagues and the singles ladder are for full members, club tournaments for summer members, and the Opening Day BBQ for Spring & Summer members. The Advanced Doubles House League is open to members and non-members. For the rest, each entry above says what is still to be confirmed.
