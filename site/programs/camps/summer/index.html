Summer 2026, for reference

Four camps, one summer.

One recreational camp for every player, and three high performance camps for children who already compete or want to. Not sure which step fits? See High Performance.

RecreationalAges 4-15

Recreational Camp

A few hours of tennis, one hour of off-court sport, a supervised lunch and snack break, and pizza lunch on Fridays.

Extras.

Summer 2026. Care and swimming were listed with the Recreational Camp; prizes ran across the camps.

  • Before and after care

    8am-9am and 4pm-5pm, $30 a day or $150 a week. Tell Josh Borenstein at 416-888-4580, one week before your camp week. For the other camps Club to confirmwhether care is offered

  • Afternoon swimming

    Optional, $15 a week, paid on the first day of the camp week.

  • Prizes every week

    Prizes at the end of every week. In 2026 one lucky camper was also to win National Bank Open tickets, to see the pros play. For 2027 Club to confirmthe prizes

Refunds.

Write to the Director and Assistant Director 3 weeks or more before your camp week starts for a full refund, less a $50 administration fee. No refund once the week has started.
