Holiday Camps 2025/26, for reference

Three camps, priced by the day.

A recreational camp for every player, and two Pro National camps for children who take tennis seriously: one for players who hope to compete, one for players already in tournaments. The prices are last winter's. 4+UTR: a Universal Tennis Rating of 4 or higher. UTR rates players from 1 to 16 on their match results.
