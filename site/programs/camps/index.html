Before you choose.

The four questions parents ask most. Every detail lives on the season pages.

Ages
Recreational Camp is for ages 4 to 15. High Performance camps run from age 5 to 18, depending on the camp and the season.
Half or full day
Every season has half and full days. Summer is booked by the week; March Break and Holiday camps are booked day by day.
Where
Summer runs at 3 locations. March Break and Holiday camps run at the club, Yonge & Centre.
