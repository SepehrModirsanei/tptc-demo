Levels and starting

Beginner 1
No playing experience.
Beginner 2
Little playing experience. Ages 14-17 have one Beginner class for little or no experience.
Intermediate 1
A 6 ball rally with a partner, 3 balls each. Ages 7-9 rally from three-quarter court; ages 10 and up from the baseline.
Intermediate 2
An 8-10 ball rally with a partner, 4-5 balls each, from the baseline. From age 10.
Advanced
Places shots with consistency: a 12-14 ball rally with a partner, 6-7 balls each, from the baseline, and gets the serve in consistently in matches. From age 10.

Lessons follow the internationally recognized Progressive Training program, so students learn in an easier format that leads to quicker success.

Group lessons begin at age 4. As there are many other activities to choose from, the primary focus at this age is to develop some fundamental skills through a fun based approach.

A racquet, a water bottle and non marking shoes. Racquets are available for students who do not have their own.

Competing, or want to? Tournament level juniors train in the High Performance program. See High Performance
