Programs

Players improve through both private and group lessons. Private lessons are a good opportunity to refine technique which can then be practiced in group lessons.

The number of lessons recommended depends on the player's developmental goals. Progress will be seen even through one group lesson per week. Additional lessons are recommended for players who are looking to play at a high club level or who have aspirations for tournament play.

Request a private lesson

Call or email with your goals, your level and the times you are free. The club also encourages you to speak to the coaches directly.
