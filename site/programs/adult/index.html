Six ways to play

From lessons to league nights. Ratings such as 3.0 or 4.5 describe playing level: the higher the number, the stronger the player.

  1. 1Group Lessons

    What
    Coached classes in multi-week sessions, for members and non-members.
    Level
    Beginner to Advanced
    When
    Evenings and weekends, by session
    Running nowSee the levels

  2. 2Private Lessons

    What
    One-to-one or semi-private, one hour, with a coach the club matches to your goals.
    Level
    Every level
    When
    Arranged with your coach
    See Private Lessons

  3. 3Round Robins

    What
    Mixed doubles: join in for a few hours of fun. Men and women welcome.
    Level
    Ages 18+
    When
    Weekday mornings and Sunday mornings
    See the round robins

  4. 4House Leagues

    What
    Doubles and singles leagues inside the club, friendly yet competitive.
    Level
    By league, from 3.0 to 4.5; A, B and C in summer
    When
    Weeknight and weekend evenings
    See the house leagues

  5. 5Interclub

    What
    Play for the club: Men's Interclub (a Majors team and an A2 team) in fall and winter, Intercounty teams in summer.
    Level
    4.5 to 5.5 for Men's Interclub; summer teams by tryout
    When
    Evenings, by team
    See interclub

  6. 6Other Adult Events

    What
    Club tournaments and the singles ladder in summer, the Opening Day BBQ, and the AGM.
    Level
    Members; tournaments by category, A to C
    When
    Through the outdoor season
    See club events

Most leagues start with membership

Several house leagues and the singles ladder are for full members, and summer tournaments for summer members. Members also book courts up to 7 days ahead and pay reduced court fees. Group lessons are open to non-members.
