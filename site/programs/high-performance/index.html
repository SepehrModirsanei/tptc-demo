Train with purpose. Compete with confidence.

A pathway in three steps, from first tournaments to provincial and national level: Little Champs, Transition Tour and Pro National.

Who it is for

For students at a provincial or national level. The focus is intensive training and the mental side of the game, with very experienced coaches who can help your player reach the next level.

The current site calls the program the Thornhill Park Tennis Academy. Club to confirmwhether Academy is the official name, and what it covers

  2. 02Transition TourAges 10-15
