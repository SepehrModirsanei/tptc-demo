Before you start

Four things to have ready before you begin.

  • New to lessons

    A quick assessment, at no charge, is recommended for every new student, so you register at the right level. Current students register at the same level unless their coach has advised a different one.

  • High Performance and Pro National

    High Performance classes need an assessment code, from the front desk after your assessment. Pro National needs pre-approval by the Head Pro.

  • Memberships

    Registered online only. Age categories apply as of January 1.

  • A card on file

    Required for all clients. Payment is by Interac, Visa or Mastercard; no cash or cheques.

How registration works

The same steps for lessons, camps and memberships. This is the only place on the site they are written out.

Have you registered with the club before?

  1. 1

    Create an account

    Choose first time registration and enter your personal information.

    Log in

    If you have taken lessons, camps or a membership with the club before, log in to your account.

  2. 2

    Open program registration

    Choose the category: group lessons, camps, or membership.

  3. 3

    Find your class

    Filter by level, age and day. Tick the class, the camp days or the membership type you want. The location is in each class name.

  4. 4

    Enter the assessment code, if asked

    Only for High Performance classes.

  5. 5

    Agree to the terms and register

    Agree to the terms and conditions at the bottom of the page, then register.

  6. 6

    Pay online

    The payment step finishes your registration. A receipt arrives by email shortly after.
