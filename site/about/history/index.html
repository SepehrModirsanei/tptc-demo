Thornhill Park Tennis Club was founded in 1951. 2026 marks seventy-five years of tennis at the club.

On record

Seventy-five years, as written down.

Only what the club has published. Solid lines carry a year from the club’s own site; dashed lines are moments we know happened, waiting for their year.

  1. 1951

    The club is founded

    Thornhill Park Tennis Club begins. Today it plays indoors in the fall and winter and outdoors in the spring and summer, at the corner of Yonge St and Centre St.

    Source: the club’s About page

  2. 19512022

    The years between

    The club’s site records no date between its founding and 2022. Founders, early members, the original courts and any earlier names belong here.

    Club to confirmFounders, the original site, and the milestones from 1951 to 2022Minutes, newsletters, old programs or photographs would let us line these years.
  3. Club to confirmYear the Dome first went up

    The Dome goes up

    Tennis at the club becomes year round: play moves under the Dome each fall and back outdoors each spring.

    Source: the club’s About page and lessons FAQ; the year is not stated

  4. Club to confirmYears the second and third locations were added

    Summer camp, one location to three

    The club’s summer camp began at one location. Two more were added in the following years because of demand, and it now runs at capacity at three.

    Source: the Head Pro’s bio on the club’s site, which says the camp began “20 years ago” but is not dated

  5. 2022

    The Majors team takes first place

    The club’s Majors team went all the way and won first place in the toughest intercounty division.

    Source: club news, 2022; the club’s photographs of the night are dated August 2022

  6. Club to confirmYear of the win; the post was last edited July 2023

    A provincial double for Pro National

    A player in the club’s Pro National Program, training with the Head Pro, won the Ontario U18 Singles and Doubles Provincial Championships.

    Club to confirmConsent to name the player
  7. 2026

    Seventy-five years

    Seventy-five years since the club was founded. The 2026/27 indoor season runs from early October to April 25, 2027.

    Founded 1951; indoor season dates from the club’s Memberships page
