On court

Court etiquette, in short.

Courts change hands on the clock, the way players change ends: on time, and between points.

  • ShoesChange out of outside shoes into on-court shoes before you step on court.
  • CrossingWait for the point to finish, then cross briskly behind the court. Return a stray ball after the point.
  • On courtWater only: no food, drinks or chewing gum.
  • NutsNo food with peanuts or traces of nuts anywhere at the club.
  • WatchingGuests watch from the east side of Court 1 only. No strollers on the courts or under the Dome.
  • PhotosNo photos or videos during programs and services, for everyone’s privacy.
