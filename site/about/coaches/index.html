How we coach

Three points, in the club’s words.

From a first lesson on a half court laid across the big one, to High Performance on the full court.

  1. 01

    Progressive Training

    The internationally recognized Progressive Training program: students learn in an easier format that leads to quicker success. The youngest start on half court, move to three-quarter court, then play the full court.

  2. 02

    Technique and tactics, every lesson

    Technical and strategic instruction is part of every lesson, with age and level appropriate games to practise skills in real situations. For young players, games are a main tool for learning the fundamentals while having fun.

  3. 03

    Club-approved coaches only

    Only coaches approved by the club may coach or teach at the club.
