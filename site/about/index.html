Who we are

Founded in 1951, Thornhill Park Tennis Club is a year-round club: indoor tennis through the fall and winter, outdoor tennis through the spring and summer.

Programming comes first. We offer group and private lessons for juniors and adults, from a first lesson to High Performance. If you simply want to play, there are memberships and a non-member option for booking a court.

  • 1951Founded. 2026 marks 75 years.
  • 4Courts, for indoor and outdoor use
  • 2Seasons: outdoors from May, under the Dome from October
