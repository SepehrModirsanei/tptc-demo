Booking rules

The rules, under six headings.

The club’s booking policies, filed under short headings. The full list is below them.

How long, how often

  • Singles (2 or 3 players): up to 1 hour.
  • Doubles (4 or more): up to 2 hours.
  • One booking a day per member.
  • Each member: 1 weeknight prime time and 1 weekend booking, plus 3 in regular time.

Cancelling

  • Cancel online at least 48 hours ahead. The member who booked cancels.
  • Later than that, or not showing up, costs the court fee, charged to the card on file.
  • The same 48 hours applies to round robins.

If it goes wrong

  • 3 no-shows, or 3 late cancels: booking can be suspended for 7 days.
  • Booking with a bot can end a membership, with no refund.

On the court

  • At most 6 balls on a court, and no ball hoppers.
  • No ball machines.

Guests

  • The same guest at most 4 times a season.
  • An unpaid guest fee is charged to the member.

Your own account

  • Members book and cancel their own courts. Staff cannot book for you.
  • The club does not guarantee court time, and may limit or cancel bookings that break these rules.

  1. Thornhill Park Tennis Club retains the right to limit any member court bookings. We also can cancel court bookings at any time for non compliance with our booking policies.
  2. Members may bring the same guest a maximum of 4 times per season.
  3. It is the responsibility of members to manage their own bookings and book or cancel their own courts. Use your court booking account login; staff are not allowed to book courts for members.
  4. The maximum duration per booking for doubles (4 people or more) is two hours a day. For singles (2 or 3 people) it is one hour a day. Maximum of one booking and court use per day per member.
  5. There is a limit of 1 booking during weeknight prime time and 1 booking on the weekend (Saturday or Sunday) per member. There is also a limit of 3 bookings during regular time (weekday day time).
  6. Members and non-members cannot use more than 6 balls on a court (no ball hoppers). Only coaches hired by the club may use more than 6 balls or ball hoppers.
  7. Ball machines are not permitted at our club.
  8. A minimum of 48 hours cancellation notice is required for court bookings and Round Robins. The member who books the court must cancel it online more than 48 hours ahead. Failure to comply results in a cancellation fee equal to the court fee or Round Robin fee.
  9. After 3 no-shows, or 3 cancellations less than 48 hours ahead, the club reserves the right to suspend booking privileges for 7 days.
  10. The use of automated tools (bots) to book courts is strictly prohibited. The club reserves the right to suspend or terminate a membership, with no refund.
  11. Thornhill Park Tennis Club does not guarantee any court time to members and guests.
  12. Once a court has been reserved and paid for, there are no refunds or rescheduling.
  13. If a guest plays with a member and does not pay the guest fee, the club charges the member.
  14. With an outstanding balance from a previous booking, you cannot make more bookings until it is settled.
  15. The credit card on file is charged the court fee for no-shows and late cancellations within 48 hours. Everyone listed on the booking is charged their share. If you tell us in advance we may open up the court to avoid the charge, but we cannot guarantee it.
  16. A credit card on file is required for all clients.

Not a member?

Friday public hours, by weekly draw.

Indoors, the club keeps 2 courts for the public on Friday nights. Places go by a random draw, one hour each.

Fridays, 9:30pm to 11:30pm Club to confirmThe hours: 9:30pm to 11:30pm, or 9pm to 11pm as a 2024 club post says

  1. 1

    Enter on Friday morning

    The form opens every Friday from 7:30am to 10am, for the next Friday. One entry per person.

  2. 2

    The draw

    The club picks players at random from the entries. Only entries made on the form count; no bookings by phone.

  3. 3

    The club calls you

    If you are picked, the club phones to tell you and to take payment, which is non-refundable.

  4. 4

    Call back by 1pm

    If you have not returned the call by 1pm, the place goes to the next player in the queue.
