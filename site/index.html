Why TPTC?

A year-round club with programs from a four year old’s first lesson to provincial and national competition.

  • Since 1951

    Founded in 1951. In 2026 the club marks 75 years.

  • Year-round tennis

    Four courts, used all year: outdoors in spring and summer, under the dome through fall and winter.

  • Programs for all ages and levels

    Group and private lessons for juniors and adults, from four year old beginners to advanced players.

  • High Performance development

    A pathway for players at a provincial or national level, from Little Champs to Pro National.

  • A strong tennis community

    House leagues, round robins, interclub teams and club events, from the Opening Day BBQ to the AGM.
