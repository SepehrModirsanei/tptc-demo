Programs11 questions

Group lessons are filed by age: 4-6, 7-9, 10-13, 14-17 and adults. For tournament level juniors there is the High Performance program, which includes Pro National. We recommend that every new student has a quick assessment, at no charge.

Find your program

Each level is a rally you can hold with a partner.

  1. Beginner 1No playing experience
  2. Beginner 2A little playing experience
  3. Intermediate 16 ball rally, 3 balls each
  4. Intermediate 28-10 ball rally, 4-5 each
  5. Advanced12-14 ball rally from the baseline, 6-7 each, and the serve in when playing matches

Current students: register at the same level unless your coach has advised a different one.

See the levels for each age

Group lessons begin at age 4. As there are many other activities to choose from, the focus at this age is to develop some fundamental skills through a fun based approach.

Junior Recreational, Ages 4-6

Students improve through both. Private lessons are a good opportunity to refine technique, which can then be practised in group lessons.

Private Lessons

Individual progress varies with natural athleticism, dedication and focus, among other things. Each student should discuss their goals with the coach, who can advise on the number and type of lessons to meet them.

Meet the coaches

Technical and strategic instruction is part of every lesson. Age and level appropriate competitive drills and games are added to practise skills in realistic situations. Games are also a primary tool for young students to develop the fundamentals while having fun.

View programs

It depends on their goals. Progress will be seen even through one group lesson a week. Additional lessons are recommended for students looking to play at a high club level, or who have aspirations for tournament play.

High Performance

Only a few players across the country are capable of playing on the professional tour. These players train multiple hours a day, 6-7 days a week.

Registration5 questions

All registration is online, in one process for lessons, camps and memberships: choose what you are registering for, create an account or log in, select your class, camp days or membership, then pay. A receipt arrives by email.

Register Now

JustBookIt (JBI) is the online registration system the club uses today for lessons, camps and memberships. If you have not registered with us before, choose first time registration and create an account with your personal information. If you have, log in to your account.

Then open program registration, choose your category and follow the steps.

How registration works

High Performance groups need an assessment code, entered when you register. Your player is assessed by the Head Pro first; the code then comes from the front desk at 905-731-5551, or by email from the Head Pro. Pro National needs the Head Pro’s pre-approval instead.

Refunds, before you register

Indoor and outdoor credits are not interchangeable: outdoor credits cannot be used for indoor programs, or the other way round. All credits expire one year after they are issued. Missed classes are not refunded or credited. If a program is cancelled, you get a full refund, or a transfer where possible.

A credit card on file is required for all clients. Payment is by Interac, Visa or Mastercard. If you have an outstanding balance from a previous booking, you cannot make more bookings until it is settled.
