Parking

Pick up a parking pass at the front desk and keep it on show: the City of Vaughan issues the tickets, not the club.

  1. Members

    A permanent pass, from the front desk. Keep it visible on the inside of your windshield at all times.

  2. Guests

    A temporary pass, from the front desk, shown the same way.

  3. Lot full

    Use the overflow: the park parking lot behind the north side of the club, down at the baseball diamond.

  4. Tickets

    The club has no jurisdiction over parking tickets. They are resolved with the City of Vaughan directly.
