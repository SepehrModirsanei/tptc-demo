Why become a member

Seven reasons, one season at a time.

The club lists seven things a membership gives you. Here they are, in the club’s order, each in one line.

  1. Court access

    The club’s four courts: outdoors from about May 1 to Sep 30, under the Dome from early October to Apr 25. No court fees outdoors, reduced court fees indoors.

  2. Advance booking

    Book up to 7 days ahead from 7:30am, where a non-member can book 1 day ahead. How court booking works

  3. Programs

    Join the club’s programs for juniors and adults, from group lessons to private coaching. View programs

  4. Leagues

    House leagues by level, the men’s interclub teams and the summer intercounty teams. See the leagues

  5. Round robins

    Mixed doubles on weekday mornings and Sunday mornings, for ages 18 and over. See the round robins

  6. Club events

    The Opening Day BBQ and round robin, club parties and the Annual General Meeting. Club to confirmDates, and what the club parties are

  7. Community

    A tennis club at Yonge & Centre since 1951, now in its 75th year.

Seasons and rates

Two seasons, one club year.

Membership is sold by the season and covers the whole season. The Dome goes up in early October and comes down after April 25.

Before you join

The fine print

The club’s own terms, for the Fall & Winter Indoor 2026/27 season.

Full season only
No partial memberships: a membership is for the full indoor season.
Intercounty only
Sunday Night Intercounty Matches Only: $90 + HST, pre-approved, no discount.
Guests
$10 per guest, each visit. The same guest at most 4 times a season. No guests during the outdoor season.
Sign-up
Online only. If you get stuck, our staff can walk you through it over the phone. Contact the club
Refunds
A full refund within 7 days of buying, less a $50 administration fee. No refunds after 7 days.
One member, one account
Memberships are not transferable. Letting a non-member book or play on your account can end a membership.
