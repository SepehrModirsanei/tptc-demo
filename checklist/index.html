Unsplash Licence

Free for commercial use and editing, no credit required. It does not cover the people in the picture: they never agreed to promote a club. Fine for a demo. We recommend none of them on the live site.

Creative Commons

CC0 has no conditions. CC BY needs a credit with the photographer, the source and the licence. CC BY-SA needs the same credit, and our edited copy is shared under the same licence. The site would carry a Photo credits line.

The club's own

Photos the club posted are the club's to use, but the copyright stays with whoever took them. A short written agreement with the photographer, and consent from the people shown, settle both.
