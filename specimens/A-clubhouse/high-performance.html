Three-quarter court, orange ball or green dot ball. Orange and green dot balls are slower, lower-bouncing balls used before the regular yellow ball.

Looking to compete, or already competing, in OTA tournaments at U9 or U10 events. OTA: the Ontario Tennis Association. U9, U10: under 9 and under 10 age groups.
