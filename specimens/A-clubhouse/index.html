Why TPTC?

  1. 1951

    Since 1951

    Playing today at the corner of Yonge & Centre. 2026 is the club's 75th year.

  2. 19.5°C

    Year-round tennis

    Outdoors in spring and summer. Under the Dome in fall and winter, kept at 19.5 °C.

  3. 4+

    Programs for all ages and levels

    Group lessons from age 4, then juniors and adults from Beginner to Advanced, members or not.

  4. 3

    High Performance development

    Three steps, Little Champs, Transition Tour and Pro National, for players at a provincial or national level.

  5. 1st

    A strong tennis community

    Round robins, house leagues, interclub and intercounty teams. The Majors team won the top intercounty division in 2022.
