Why TPTC?

Since

1951

75 years in 2026

Founded in 1951. Today the club plays at the corner of Yonge St and Centre St, Thornhill.

  • Year-round tennis

    Under the Dome in Fall & Winter, outdoors in Spring & Summer, on four courts.

  • Programs for all ages and levels

    Group lessons from age 4, private lessons for juniors and adults, Beginner to Advanced.

  • High Performance development

    A competitive pathway from Little Champs to the Pro National Academy, for provincial and national level players.

1stMajors team, the toughest intercounty division, 2022

A strong tennis community

Round robins, house leagues, interclub and intercounty teams.
