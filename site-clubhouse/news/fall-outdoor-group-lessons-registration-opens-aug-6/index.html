Fall outdoor recreational group lessons: registration opens Aug 6 at 10am

Registration for the end of August and September outdoor session opens Thursday, August 6 at 10am. The session runs 7 weeks from August 21, at Yonge and Centre.

Posted
Aug 5, 2026
Written by
Thornhill Park Tennis Club

Registration closed It closed on Oct 8, 2026.

See Junior Recreational

Registration for our end of August and September session of outdoor recreational group lessons will be live on our website on August 6 at 10am!

We are offering lessons at our Yonge and Centre club. This outdoor session will run from August 21 for a total of 7 weeks.

We are offering recreational group lessons (Beginner 1/2, Intermediate 1/2, Advanced) for all ages: kids, teenagers and adults!

Should you have any questions, or if you would like to schedule an assessment, please contact the Head Pro, Josh Borenstein, at jborenstein26@live.ca.

We can’t wait to see you all there!
